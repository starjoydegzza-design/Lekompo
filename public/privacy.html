Privacy Policy

Best Lekompo Mixtapes does not require personal accounts. Shared mixtapes, nominee details, artwork and vote records are stored in the connected database and file-storage service so the same information is available to visitors across devices.

Browser storage and votes

The site uses browser storage for a mixtape cache and to remember weekly mixtape voting state. The DJ of the Year election uses a random, HTTP-only browser cookie to limit voting to one vote per browser. A one-way hash of that cookie is stored with the vote; it is not a name, email address or account profile. The election cookie can remain on the browser for up to one year. Clearing browser storage or cookies may affect these browser-level controls.

Admin sessions

Admin pages use a secure, HTTP-only session cookie after password sign-in. The session expires automatically after eight hours and can also be ended by logging out.

Third-party video

Mixes may be embedded from services such as YouTube or Vimeo. When a video loads, those services may set their own cookies and collect data according to their own privacy policies.
