Privacy Policy

Best Lekompo Mixtapes keeps things simple. We do not run a backend server and we do not collect personal accounts.

What we store

The list of mixtapes and your votes are saved locally in your own browser using localStorage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Votes

To keep voting fair, we remember which mixes you have voted for on this device. Clearing your browser storage will reset this.

Third-party video

Mixes may be embedded from services such as YouTube or Vimeo. When a video loads, those services may set their own cookies and collect data according to their own privacy policies.
