About

Best Lekompo Mixtapes is a community voting spot for the hottest Lekompo mixes. Watch the mixes, then cast your vote for the ones that hit the hardest.

Every mix on the homepage is ranked by the number of votes it receives. The more love a mix gets, the higher it climbs the list.

How voting works

Each device can vote once per mix. Votes are stored locally so you can come back and see how your favourites are doing.
